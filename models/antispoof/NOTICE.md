# Anti-spoofing model weights

`MiniFASNetV2.fp32.onnx` and `MiniFASNetV1SE.fp32.onnx` are ONNX conversions of the
pretrained MiniFASNet weights from Minivision's
[Silent-Face-Anti-Spoofing](https://github.com/minivision-ai/Silent-Face-Anti-Spoofing),
licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Regenerate them with `python ml/antispoof/export_onnx.py`.
